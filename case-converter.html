Students use it to hit essay word count requirements. Bloggers use it to check article length before publishing. Freelancers use it to ensure proposals meet client specifications. Word Count Guidelines for Different Content Types

Blog posts typically perform best between 1,500 and 2,500 words for SEO purposes. University essays vary by institution but most require between 500 and 5,000 words. LinkedIn posts perform best under 300 words. Twitter is limited to 280 characters. Email subject lines should stay under 50 characters. Instagram captions work best between 100 and 150 words.
