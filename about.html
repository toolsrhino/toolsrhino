Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% of a number?

Use the first calculator on this page. Enter 20 in the percentage field and your number in the second field. The result appears instantly. For example 20% of 500 = 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Use the "Percentage Change" calculator. Enter your starting value in the "From" field and your new value in the "To" field. A positive result means increase, a negative result means decrease.

How do I calculate a discount?

Use the "Discount Calculator" at the bottom of the page. Enter the original price and the discount percentage. The calculator shows you the final price after the discount is applied.

How do I calculate sales tax?

Use the first calculator. Enter your tax rate percentage and your pre-tax amount. The result is the tax amount. Add this to your original amount to get the total with tax.
