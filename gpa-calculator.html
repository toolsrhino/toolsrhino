Frequently Asked Questions

How is my monthly loan payment calculated?

Monthly payment is calculated using the standard amortization formula which considers your loan amount, monthly interest rate and number of payments. Our calculator does this automatically — just enter your loan details and see your payment instantly.

What is an amortization schedule?

An amortization schedule shows every payment over the life of your loan broken down into principal and interest. In early payments most of your money goes to interest. Over time more goes to paying down the principal. Our calculator shows the full schedule for every month of your loan.

How can I reduce the total interest I pay?

You can reduce total interest by making extra payments toward the principal, choosing a shorter loan term, or refinancing at a lower interest rate. Use our calculator to compare different scenarios by changing the term and rate fields.

Does this work for Pakistani bank loans?

Yes. Enter the loan amount in PKR, your bank's annual interest rate, and the loan term in years. The calculator works for any currency and any loan type.
