Free GPA Calculator — Calculate Your GPA Instantly

Our free GPA calculator makes it easy to calculate your Grade Point Average on a 4.0 scale. Add your courses, credit hours and grades and your GPA updates automatically. Perfect for university students in Pakistan, the US, and anywhere that uses a 4.0 grading system.

The calculator uses weighted GPA calculation — meaning courses with more credit hours have a greater impact on your overall GPA. This is the standard method used by most universities worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credit hours, adding all those values together, then dividing by the total number of credit hours. For example an A in a 3-credit course gives you 4.0 x 3 = 12 quality points. Our calculator does all of this automatically.

What GPA do I need for Dean's List?

Most universities require a GPA of 3.5 or higher for Dean's List recognition. Some require 3.7 or above. Our calculator shows you a status badge indicating whether you are on track for Dean's List based on your current GPA.

What is a good GPA in Pakistan?

In Pakistani universities that use the 4.0 scale, a GPA above 3.5 is considered excellent, 3.0-3.5 is good, 2.5-3.0 is average, and below 2.0 may put you at academic risk. Requirements vary by university and program.

Can I calculate my cumulative GPA?

Yes. Add all your courses from all semesters into the calculator. Include the course name, credit hours and grade for each. The calculator will automatically compute your cumulative weighted GPA across all entries.
