How to Use the Number to Words Converter

Simply type any number in the input field above and the words appear instantly. This tool is especially useful when writing amounts on cheques, bank drafts, invoices or any legal document where numbers must be written in words to prevent fraud.

The converter handles numbers up to billions and correctly formats amounts with "and" between hundreds and smaller units as required in formal English writing.

Why do cheques need amounts in words?+
Writing the amount in words on a cheque prevents fraud and tampering. If only numbers were written, someone could easily add a digit to change the amount. Words are much harder to alter without detection.
Does it support Pakistani Rupees format?+
Yes — the cheque format shows the amount with "Rupees" and "Paisa" when you include decimal values, which is the standard format used on Pakistani bank cheques.
What is the maximum number it can convert?+
The converter handles numbers up to 999 billion, which covers virtually all practical use cases including large business invoices and financial documents.
