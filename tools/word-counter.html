How to Use the Word Counter

Simply type or paste your text into the box above and all statistics update instantly — no need to click any button. The tool counts your words, characters (with and without spaces), number of sentences, and even estimates how long it will take to read your text.

This free word counter is perfect for writers, students, bloggers, freelancers, and anyone who needs to track the length of their content. Use it for essays, articles, social media posts, emails, proposals, and more.

Why Use ToolsRhino Word Counter?

Unlike other word counters, ToolsRhino is completely free with no signup required. Your text stays private — we never store or send your text anywhere. Everything runs locally in your browser for maximum speed and privacy.
