Frequently Asked Questions

How much tip should I leave?

Standard tipping etiquette in most countries: 15% for average service, 18-20% for good service, 25% or more for exceptional service. In Pakistan tipping is less common but 10-15% is appreciated at upscale restaurants.

How do I split a bill between friends?

Enter the total bill amount, select your tip percentage, then enter the number of people splitting the bill. The calculator instantly shows how much each person pays including their tip share.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

It is generally considered appropriate to tip on the pre-tax amount since you are tipping for the service, not the government's portion. However tipping on the full amount is also perfectly acceptable and common.
