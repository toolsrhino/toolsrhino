Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my invoice as a PDF?

Click the "Print / Save as PDF" button. In your browser's print dialog, look for the "Destination" or "Printer" option and select "Save as PDF". Then click Save. Your professional invoice will download as a PDF file instantly.

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no credit card, no subscription. ToolsRhino invoice generator is completely free to use as many times as you want forever.

Can I add tax to my invoice?

Yes. In the items section there is a tax percentage field. Enter your tax rate and the tax amount and final total will calculate automatically.

Are my invoice details saved?

No. Your invoice data is not stored anywhere. Everything stays in your browser. Once you close or refresh the page the data is cleared. We recommend saving your PDF before closing the page.

Can I use this for Pakistani clients and PKR currency?

Yes. You can enter any currency symbol or amount in the rate fields. Simply type PKR or Rs before your amounts in the description or adjust the total accordingly. The tool works for any currency worldwide.
