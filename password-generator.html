Why Use a Password Generator?

Weak passwords are the number one cause of account breaches. Using a random password generator ensures your passwords are truly unpredictable — unlike passwords based on names, dates or common words that hackers can easily guess.

This password generator runs entirely in your browser. The passwords are generated using your device's cryptographic random number generator and are never sent to any server, never logged and never stored. It is completely private and secure.

How long should my password be?+
Security experts recommend a minimum of 12 characters. 16 characters provides strong security for most accounts. For highly sensitive accounts like banking or email, use 20+ characters with all character types enabled.
Are the generated passwords safe?+
Yes. Passwords are generated using the browser's built-in cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues). They are never sent to our servers, never stored and only exist in your browser memory.
Should I use a different password for every account?+
Absolutely yes. If you reuse a password and one account is breached, all your accounts become vulnerable. Use a unique password for every account and store them in a trusted password manager like Bitwarden or 1Password.
